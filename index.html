1. Privacy Policy (GDPR & Swiss FADP)

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Fridge AI (Android Package: com.pocketfridge.ai) collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile application.

1.1 Data Controller / Contact

Operator: Mladen Jakovljevic
Address: Lucerne, Switzerland
Support & Privacy Email: pocketfridge.support@gmail.com

1.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your pantry inventory, weekly meal planner, shopping lists, dietary preferences, and saved recipes are primarily stored locally on your device (using local browser storage / IndexedDB). We do not sell this data or use it for third-party advertising.

1.3 Data Processed via Third-Party Services

  • Authentication & Cloud Credits (Supabase): When you sign in with Google, we process your User ID, email address, and display name via Supabase (Supabase Inc.) solely to authenticate your account and securely manage your AI recipe & receipt scan credit balance.
  • AI Recipe Generation & Receipt Scanning (Google Gemini API): When you generate a recipe or scan a grocery receipt, your ingredient list, preferences, or receipt photo are transmitted securely via our backend proxy to the Google Gemini API (Google LLC) to generate recipes, food images, or extract grocery items. Receipt photos are processed transiently and are not stored permanently on our servers or used for advertising profiles.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing & RevenueCat): Optional credit packages are processed via Google Play and managed via RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.). We do not have access to your credit card or banking details; we only receive anonymized purchase confirmation tokens to credit your account.

1.4 Your Rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP / revDSG), you have the right to request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us at pocketfridge.support@gmail.com.

1.5 Children's Privacy

Pocket Fridge AI is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

2. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Pocket Fridge AI account and all associated cloud data at any time.

Option A – Instant Deletion directly in the App:
  1. Open the Pocket Fridge AI app.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear icon) in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Permanently Delete Account & Cloud Data".
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account, email address, and credit balance are immediately removed from our database.

Option B – Deletion via Email: If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from the address linked to your account to pocketfridge.support@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request – Pocket Fridge AI". We will delete your account and cloud data within 7 business days.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained?

  • Deleted immediately: Your Supabase user account, linked email address, display name, and remaining AI credit balance. Local pantry/recipe data is cleared on the device.
  • Retained by third parties (legal requirement): Google Play retains payment transaction records as required by statutory tax and accounting laws.

2. Konto & Daten dauerhaft löschen

Du kannst dein Pocket-Fridge-AI-Konto und alle zugehörigen Cloud-Daten jederzeit vollständig löschen.

Option A – Sofortige Löschung direkt in der App:
  1. Öffne die App Pocket Fridge AI.
  2. Tippe oben rechts auf das Zahnrad-Symbol (Einstellungen).
  3. Scrolle ganz nach unten und tippe auf „Konto & Datenbank-Daten dauerhaft löschen“.
  4. Bestätige die Sicherheitsabfrage. Dein Konto, deine E-Mail-Adresse und dein Guthaben werden sofort unwiderruflich aus unserer Datenbank gelöscht.

Option B – Löschung per E-Mail: Falls du keinen Zugriff mehr auf die App hast, sende uns eine E-Mail von deiner registrierten Adresse an pocketfridge.support@gmail.com mit dem Betreff „Kontolöschung – Pocket Fridge AI“. Wir löschen dein Konto innerhalb von 7 Werktagen.
